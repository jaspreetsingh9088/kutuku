Information we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number (used to log in via OTP), and optionally your name, email address and GSTIN.
  • Delivery addresses you save for orders.
  • Order details: products purchased, amounts, order status and invoices.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive a payment reference and status; we do not receive or store your card, UPI PIN or bank credentials.
  • Device notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send order updates and, if you allow, offers.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, process and deliver orders, issue GST invoices and provide customer support.
  • To send order confirmation, dispatch and delivery notifications, and promotional notifications you can turn off in your phone's settings.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run the service: Razorpay (payments), our SMS provider (OTP delivery), Google Firebase (notifications), our hosting provider, and delivery partners (name, phone and address for delivery). We do not sell your personal data.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete my account in the app, or by emailing [support email]. This removes your profile, addresses, cart and wishlist. Order and invoice records are kept for the period required by Indian tax law.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on secured servers with restricted access and daily backups.
